# Fact-check log: 5 October 2026

This log checks the seed data and technical claims in `eat-the-world-london-brief.md`.

## Method

- **Postcodes:** every seed postcode was looked up with the postcodes.io bulk API. Changed postcodes were re-resolved, and their distance from the old postcode was measured.
- **Restaurants:** 8 web-research agents (Sonnet, medium effort) each checked one regional batch: trading status, name, address, postcode, specialist fit, and claims in the curator note. Every proposed change was then re-checked by an independent agent (Sonnet, high effort) told to refute it.
- **Stack and legal claims:** 2 research agents (Sonnet) checked 28 claims against primary sources. An Opus sceptic re-checked every decision-critical or less-than-certain claim.
- **Signature dishes:** drafted by Haiku, then reviewed and corrected by hand (see the end of this log).
- **Google place IDs could not be verified.** That needs the Places API, which the brief rules out. Maps links fall back to name and address search, so a stale ID is harmless.

**Result:** 69 rows checked. 68 kept across 39 countries, 1 removed, and 41 with a name, address, postcode or confidence change. Every kept row has its curator note rewritten from the evidence.
